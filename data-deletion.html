Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: September 23, 2026

You can request deletion of data associated with your FlowPilot Instagram integration at any time.

Option 1 — Disconnect FlowPilot from Instagram

Open Instagram and go to the section that manages connected apps and websites. Locate FlowPilot and remove its access. This revokes FlowPilot's authorization to access the connected Instagram account through Meta's APIs.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

Send an email to saifeddineelmezdari@gmail.com with the subject FlowPilot Data Deletion Request.

Please include enough information to identify the connected FlowPilot account, such as the Instagram username used with the service. Do not send passwords or access tokens.

What we delete

After validating the request, we will delete or anonymize FlowPilot-held data associated with the integration that is no longer required to operate the service or meet legal, fraud-prevention or security obligations. This may include stored integration identifiers, automation-related records and stored authorization data.

Timing

We aim to process valid deletion requests within 30 days. Where a longer period is required by law or for technical reasons, we will communicate the applicable status when possible.

Contact

For questions about deletion, contact saifeddineelmezdari@gmail.com.
