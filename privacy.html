FlowPilot

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

FlowPilot is an automation tool designed to help professional Instagram accounts manage interactions such as comments and direct messages. This Privacy Policy explains what information we process, why we process it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information we process

When an Instagram professional account connects to FlowPilot, we may process information made available through Meta's APIs, including:

  • Instagram professional account identifiers and basic profile information;
  • comments, comment identifiers, usernames and related post information when needed to trigger an automation;
  • direct-message events and message identifiers when needed to provide messaging automations;
  • access tokens and authorization information required to connect the account;
  • technical logs used to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service.

2. How we use information

We use this information only to provide FlowPilot features requested by the connected account, including detecting automation triggers, sending configured replies, maintaining integrations, preventing abuse, and diagnosing technical problems.

3. Meta and Instagram data

FlowPilot accesses Instagram data only through authorized Meta APIs and only after the account owner grants the required permissions. We do not sell Instagram user data. Access tokens are treated as confidential credentials and are used only to provide the connected integration.

4. Data sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers used to operate FlowPilot, such as hosting and database services, only as necessary to provide the service or comply with legal obligations.

5. Data retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain security and logs, or meet legal requirements. Connected-account data may be deleted when an integration is disconnected or upon a valid deletion request, subject to limited technical or legal retention requirements.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect credentials and service data. No internet-based system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and deletion requests

You may disconnect FlowPilot from Instagram through your Instagram or Meta account settings. You may also request deletion of data associated with your FlowPilot integration by following our Data Deletion Instructions.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as FlowPilot evolves. The date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.
